<p class="document-date">August 2026 · Journal issue · IEEE ESL</p>

This scheduling study combines software planning with hardware decisions at runtime. A domain-specific language describes task dependencies for offline analysis; a hardware scheduler checks readiness and dispatches tasks according to execution status. The approach addresses responsiveness, parallelism and programmability in latency-sensitive workloads.
