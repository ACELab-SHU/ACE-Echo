Yule Jiao, Shiji Ruan, Limin Jiang, Zhiyuan Jiang, Shan Cao

2026 IEEE International Symposium on Circuits and Systems (ISCAS) · Live Demonstrations: RF

This work contributes to research around Venus. For supported platform applications and measured results, see the [ACE-Echo 1.0 validation scope](venus1_validation.html).
