<p class="document-date">September 2026 · Journal issue · IEEE TCAD</p>

Zoozve explores arbitrary register grouping to handle long vectors without repeated software strip-mining. The work brings together instruction-set design, LLVM compilation and a hardware prototype, addressing register allocation and vector data movement as parts of one system.

## Publication

**An Arbitrary Register Grouping Scheme for RISC-V Vector Extension: Compilation Support and Hardware Implementation**

Limin Jiang, Siyi Xu, Yintao Liu, Yihao Shen, Yi Shi, Shan Cao, Zhiyuan Jiang

IEEE Transactions on Computer-Aided Design of Integrated Circuits and Systems, 45(9), 4450-4463

This work contributes to research around Venus. For supported platform applications and measured results, see the [ACE-Echo 1.0 validation scope](venus1_validation.html).
